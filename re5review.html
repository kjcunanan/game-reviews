Resident Evil 5

Game Review: First off, wow! I’ve always wanted to play a resident evil game and this was my first one! Before anything, it’s important to note that my sister and I played this game together co-op style on the playstation. I played as Chris and her Sheva. I’ve personally played on the playstation for a big chunk of my life and found the controls to be difficult, at first, to get used to. However, after understanding them I found I really began to enjoy combat. The options on all the weapons feels fresh and there never was a moment where I felt underwhelmed with the newer enemies and utility. I think the one issue felt was the lack of ammo available for certain guns which is understandable but can feel frustrating at times, especially because you’re left with a tiny knife. When it comes to music I found the boss's music to feel really action packed, specifically loved the final boss one. However, at other times I found my sister and I to lower the music because of how distracting it can be during waves of combat. Additionally, we found ourselves replaying a few levels specifically for ammo which was frustrating especially because of how repetitive it felt at times. Overall, we enjoyed the game and story but probably wouldn’t play it again.
